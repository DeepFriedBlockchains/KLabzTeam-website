Development update

Our original development schedule targeted the first public release in September 2026. However, unexpected source-code and compatibility issues caused the project to fall behind schedule.

After resolving the initial development issues, the team planned a limited soft release while awaiting Tari’s upcoming Ootle network upgrade.

With the upgrade approaching, the team determined that delaying publication to address the new Tari software and compatibility requirements would provide a better foundation for the application’s initial release.

Development continues as we prepare the miner for compatibility with the updated Tari ecosystem.
